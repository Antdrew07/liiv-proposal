# LIIV Atlanta Proposal — Design Brainstorm

## Context
A high-graphics landing page proposal for social media management services offered to LIIV Atlanta Church.
Client: LIIV Atlanta | Pastor Mayo Sowell | $3,500/month package

<idea>

**Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalist Gospel — Raw power meets divine purpose

**Core Principles:**
1. Oversized, collision-based typography that commands attention
2. High-contrast black/yellow/green palette mirroring LIIV's existing identity
3. Diagonal cuts and overlapping sections that feel kinetic and alive
4. Unpolished edges with intentional roughness — authentic, not corporate

**Color Philosophy:** Black as the foundation of authority, LIIV yellow (#F5C200) as divine light breaking through, electric green (#39FF14) as the pulse of life and growth. The contrast is jarring but intentional — this is a church that disrupts.

**Layout Paradigm:** Stacked horizontal bands with aggressive diagonal slices between sections. No centered grids. Content bleeds to edges. Numbers and stats are massive — treated as graphic elements.

**Signature Elements:**
- Giant outlined numerals as background texture (e.g., "$3,500" spanning full width)
- Thick black borders and offset shadow boxes on cards
- Yellow highlight bars cutting through dark sections

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states feel like a punch — elements shift, borders thicken, colors invert. Scroll-triggered reveals that feel like curtains being torn away.

**Animation:** Staggered text reveals where each word drops in from above. Section transitions use clip-path wipes. Stats count up on scroll entry.

**Typography System:** "Bebas Neue" for all display text (ultra-condensed, all-caps), "DM Sans" for body copy. Extreme size contrast — 120px headlines vs 16px body.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin gold horizontal rules between sections
- Large ampersands and em-dashes as decorative punctuation
- Circular photo frames with gold borders

**Interaction Philosophy:** Slow, deliberate hover transitions. Elements breathe. Nothing is rushed. The experience mirrors the gravitas of the subject matter.

**Animation:** Fade-in with upward drift on scroll. Gold lines draw themselves in. Pricing number rolls up like a counter.

**Typography System:** "Playfair Display" for headlines (editorial, authoritative), "Lato" for body. Mixed case with strategic all-caps for labels.

<idea>

**Design Movement:** Urban Gospel Manifesto — Atlanta street culture meets divine mission

**Core Principles:**
1. Bold, unapologetic color blocking in LIIV's exact palette (black, yellow, coral, green)
2. Oversized section numbers as wayfinding elements
3. Layered textures — concrete, grain, light leaks — that feel Atlanta
4. Proposal structured like a magazine editorial, not a business document

**Color Philosophy:** LIIV's exact colors: near-black (#0D0D0D), vibrant yellow (#F5C200), coral/terracotta (#E85D3A), electric green (#2ECC40). Each section gets its own dominant color, creating a visual journey through the proposal.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed sections with offset content blocks. Services displayed in a large editorial card grid. Pricing section is a full-screen moment with the $3,500 number treated as a hero graphic. No centered layouts — everything is intentionally off-axis.

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) in a contrasting color
- Diagonal stripe accents (yellow/black) as dividers
- Bold pill badges for service tags

**Interaction Philosophy:** Scroll-triggered color shifts where the background transitions between the brand colors as you move through sections. Hover on service cards reveals details with a slide-up animation.

**Animation:** Clip-path reveal animations on scroll. Numbers count up. Color transitions between sections are smooth but dramatic.

**Typography System:** "Barlow Condensed" for display (bold, condensed, urban), "IBM Plex Sans" for body (technical, trustworthy). Heavy use of uppercase for headings.

## Selected Design: Urban Gospel Manifesto

Chosen for its direct alignment with LIIV Atlanta's existing bold, high-energy visual identity. The color-blocked sections, editorial layout, and oversized typography will make this proposal feel like an extension of the church's brand — not a generic business document. The $3,500 pricing moment will feel earned and impactful.
